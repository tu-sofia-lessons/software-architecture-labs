Title: Software Architecture
Updated: 2026-02-01

Software Architecture (SA101) is taught by Prof. Stoyanova. The course has weekly lectures and ten practical labs.

To pass Software Architecture, students must attend at least 70% of the practical labs and achieve a final grade of at least 3.00.

The final grade is made of 40% practical labs and 60% final exam. The final exam has 30 multiple-choice questions and lasts 90 minutes.

AI tools are allowed in the labs, but every student must defend their lab work orally.
